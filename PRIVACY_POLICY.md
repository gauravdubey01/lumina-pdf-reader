### Overview

Thank you for choosing **OmniPDF**. Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles regarding the collection, storage, and processing of your information while using the OmniPDF desktop application on Windows.

**In summary: OmniPDF does not collect, harvest, store, transmit, or sell any of your personal data or document content. All operations are performed 100% locally on your PC.**

---

### 1. Information Collection & Storage

- **No Personal Data Collection:** OmniPDF does not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.
- **Local Document Processing:** All document interactions—including opening, reading, annotating, searching, highlighting, drawing, merging, splitting, rotating, exporting, and password-protecting PDFs—are executed entirely offline on your local device using embedded client-side libraries.
- **Zero Document Uploads:** Your files, document metadata, and text contents are never transmitted, uploaded, or synced to any external server, cloud infrastructure, or third party.

---

### 2. Features & Network Usage

- **Webpage to PDF Conversion:** If you explicitly choose to use the optional *Convert Webpage to PDF* feature, OmniPDF will make an outbound HTTP/HTTPS request to the specific public URL you provided to fetch and render that webpage into a local PDF. OmniPDF does not log, track, or share your requested URLs or browsing habits.
- **External Links (Ko-fi & Web Links):** When you click on the optional support link (*"Support on Ko-fi"*) or external tutorial documentation, your default web browser opens the destination page (`https://ko-fi.com/gauravdubeypro`). Interaction with external websites is governed by their respective privacy policies.
- **No Analytics or Telemetry:** OmniPDF does not contain telemetry trackers, third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or behavior monitoring tools.

---

### 3. Application Permissions

OmniPDF uses the standard Windows Desktop Full Trust capability (`runFullTrust`) in its Microsoft Store package manifest. This permission is utilized exclusively to:
- Access files and directories that you explicitly choose to open, read, or save.
- Read and save your local user preferences (such as your chosen theme, view mode, zoom settings, and recent files list stored locally in the Windows Registry / AppData).
- Send print jobs to your locally configured Windows printers when you initiate a print action.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy

Because OmniPDF does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information whatsoever, it is fully compliant with privacy regulations worldwide, including the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). OmniPDF is completely safe for users of all ages.
