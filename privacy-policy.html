Privacy Policy for OmniPDF

Effective Date: September 29, 2026
Application: OmniPDF
Developer / Publisher: Gaurav Dubey (Gaurav_dubey)
Summary: OmniPDF does not collect, harvest, store, transmit, or sell any of your personal data or document content. All operations are performed 100% locally and offline on your computer.

1. Information Collection & Storage

  • No Personal Data Collection: OmniPDF does not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • Local Document Processing: All document interactions—including opening, reading, annotating, searching, highlighting, drawing, merging, splitting, rotating, exporting, and password-protecting PDFs—are executed entirely offline on your local device.
  • Zero Document Uploads: Your files, document metadata, and text contents are never transmitted, uploaded, or synced to any external server or third party.

2. Features & Network Usage

  • Webpage to PDF Conversion: If you explicitly choose to use the optional Convert Webpage to PDF feature, OmniPDF makes an outbound HTTP/HTTPS request to the specific public URL you provide solely to fetch and render that page into a local PDF. OmniPDF does not log or share your requested URLs.
  • External Links: When clicking external links (e.g. "Support on Ko-fi"), your default web browser opens the destination page. Interaction with external websites is governed by their respective privacy policies.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: OmniPDF does not contain telemetry trackers, third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or behavior monitoring tools.

3. Application Permissions

OmniPDF utilizes the standard Windows Desktop Full Trust capability (runFullTrust) solely to:

  • Access files and directories that you explicitly choose to open, read, or save.
  • Read and save your local user preferences (such as your chosen theme, view mode, zoom settings, and recent files list stored locally in your Windows Registry / AppData).
  • Send print jobs to your locally configured Windows printers when you initiate printing.

4. Children’s Privacy

Because OmniPDF does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information whatsoever, it is fully compliant with privacy regulations worldwide, including COPPA and GDPR. OmniPDF is completely safe for users of all ages.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect future software enhancements or changes in legal regulations. Any revisions will be published directly to this page with an updated date.
